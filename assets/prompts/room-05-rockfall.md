# 5번 방 채석장 낙석 스프라이트

5번 방 게임 화면을 스타일 참고로 제공하고 내장 ImageGen으로 낙석 바닥·바위·파편을 한 장의 [투명 시트](../maps/room-05/rockfall-props.png)에 생성했습니다.

```text
Create a game sprite atlas of three isolated transparent cutouts for the attached 2D Pico2D quarry ruins game. The image of the game is a REFERENCE for camera and texture only. Three art pieces arranged horizontally, one in each equal third, ample blank transparent gutters, no overlap. LEFT: a low ruined quarry impact bed built into a floor tile, with chipped warm brown masonry edging, a relatively flat dark patch of rough ochre shale and scattered existing small rubble, square plan, viewed from directly above with slightly visible horizontal south lip. It will be tiled into a large rectangle and must look rough and worn but flat enough to walk on. CENTER: one large asymmetric falling sandstone boulder, strong angular silhouette with amber clay colored warm lit facets, darker brown fracture lines and small desaturated slate faces, hand-painted chunky pixel-textured match to the walls and earth in the reference, no ground and no shadow. RIGHT: a low impact debris cluster consisting of three to five separated angular broken ochre rock shards over a small faint soft warm gray dust tuft, wider than tall, suitable for sitting flat on paving where a boulder struck. All three cutouts use stone hues found in the attached game, clean Alpha around each, slight stylized pixel art texture, silhouettes clear at 25 to 70 pixels. No labels, characters, UI, text, arrows, symbols, screenshot, sky, background, or frames. The sprite sheet itself is returned isolated on a truly transparent background. Edges of impact bed aligned horizontally and vertically with the reference map tiles.
```

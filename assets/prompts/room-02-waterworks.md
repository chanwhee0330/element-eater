## 프롬프트

> Generate a production pixel-art game PROP ATLAS for two objects in the supplied Korean top-down dungeon game. Reference image is STRICTLY for color and perspective only. Genuinely transparent background, tight isolated hard-edged sprites, NO screenshot, floor plane, text, UI, characters, or shadow beyond silhouette. Canvas landscape 3:2. Put exactly TWO objects far apart in two columns, each centered in its own half, with a fully transparent empty gutter at the center and ample transparent padding at the edges. LEFT PROP: small ancient square lavastone steam drain tile, 1:1 footprint, steep front-facing top-down 2D perspective aligned to screen, a shallow muted dusty-mauve stone rim with tightly fitted dark openings and four old brown bronze slats, two subtle amber glass warning lenses in visible corners, near-flat with only a 2 pixel beveled front lip, clean strong shape for 46x30 px final display. No smoke, no glow, no emissions. RIGHT PROP: tall WIDE medieval dungeon cistern constructed from carved dusty purple-pink squared ashlar blocks, strong frontal symmetric silhouette with no diagonal isometric edges. Opening at about 43% of sprite height, centered horizontally on front wall below an open-topped subtle deep blue pool. The centered arched mouth has a recessed dark cavity where an animated shutter will be composited, roughly one third of width and one quarter of height. Flanking structural square piers, visible front brick wall extending BELOW the mouth, worn thin teal patina around opening, no bright tones. Slightly more highly detailed true hand-placed pixel art at around 150x120 perceived px, dark accessible outlines, deliberately muted 16-bit illumination with dithered stone texture matching attached game. Horizontal alignment and pixel art, nearest neighbor chunky quality. NO moving water outside the cistern: flow will be animated by renderer. Neither sprite touches the other. No extra props or panels.

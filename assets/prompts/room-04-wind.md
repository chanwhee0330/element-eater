## 프롬프트

> Use the attached Element Eater room 04 screenshot for its art style and materials ONLY. Generate one landscape isolated pixel-art sprite atlas on truly transparent alpha, three completely separate assets with large clear transparent gutters, no page, floor outside sprites, shading drops outside the asset, text, UI, player, grid or diagram. Left third upper-middle: ONE ancient square inset floor WIND VENT, steep square-aligned top-down slightly visible front lip, same muted blue-violet slate as the screenshot blockers but with a very thin subdued sage-bronze rim so it reads as a flush hazard tile. Three broad horizontal curved iron louvers slightly shaped like blowing wind, dark recessed air slots, one small inlaid muted gray-green swirl mark, fine stone chipping and four low-profile bronze rivets; readable at 50 by 30 game pixels. No emitted wind, particles or light on the vent. Right two thirds upper half: ONE isolated elongated thin mist ribbon with horizontally stretched soft pale dusty blue-lavender cloud wisps on transparency, viewed from the same top-down camera, organic hand-pixelled airy feathered transparent edges that taper entirely away on all sides, staggered multiple shallow wisp striations with softly mottled transparent holes. Right two thirds lower half: a SECOND distinct isolated softer, wider and thinner drift of the same mist with denser ribbon at one end and almost totally transparent trailing end, not a cloud ball. Keep the two mist sprites physically separate and clear alpha between them. Both mist sprites have a tiny amount of pixel-art dithering with smooth opacity falloff at edges; no rectangle or hard flat edge, no ground, sky, stars, cliff or scenery, no white cartoon smoke outlines. The reference game has warm stone pavers and cool violet stonework floating over a black-purple abyss; art must be readable and cohesive with that subdued palette.
